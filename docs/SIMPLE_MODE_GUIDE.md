# 🎯 Simple Mode - User Guide

## Welcome to Vive Code Simple Mode!

Simple Mode is designed for **absolute beginners** who want to create projects **without writing any code**. Just describe what you want to build, and our AI will create it for you!

---

## 🚀 Quick Start (3 Steps)

### Step 1: Describe Your Idea
On the homepage, you'll see a big text box asking: **"What do you want to build today?"**

Type your idea in simple words:
- ✅ "Create a calculator"
- ✅ "Make a todo list app"
- ✅ "Build a personal website"
- ✅ "Create a quiz game"

**The more details you add, the better!** For example:
- Instead of: "Make a calculator"
- Try: "Create a calculator with a blue background that can add, subtract, multiply and divide numbers"

### Step 2: Click "Create My Project"
After typing your idea, click the big blue button. Our AI will:
1. Understand your idea
2. Write all the code for you
3. Create a working project in seconds!

### Step 3: See Your Project Live!
You'll be taken to the editor where you can:
- **See your project** working in real-time
- **Test it** by clicking buttons and using features
- **Share it** with friends by clicking the Share button

---

## 💬 Using the AI Assistant

Once your project is created, you have a friendly AI assistant on the right side of your screen.

### How to Ask the AI for Changes

Just type what you want in plain English:

#### **Changing Colors**
- "Make the background blue"
- "Change text color to red"
- "Use purple buttons"

#### **Adding Features**
- "Add a reset button"
- "Include a counter"
- "Add sound effects"

#### **Changing Size/Layout**
- "Make the text bigger"
- "Center everything"
- "Make it full screen"

#### **Adding Text**
- "Add a title that says 'My Calculator'"
- "Include instructions"
- "Add a welcome message"

### Quick Action Buttons

Can't think of what to ask? Click the quick action buttons:
- **Change colors** - AI will suggest new color schemes
- **Add a button** - Adds a new button to your project
- **Make it bigger** - Increases sizes
- **Add animation** - Makes things move and look cool!

---

## 👀 Viewing Your Project

### Preview Mode (Default)
This shows your project exactly as it will look when you share it. Click buttons, type in boxes, and test everything!

### View Code
Curious about how it works? Click "View Code" to see the actual code. Don't worry - you don't need to understand it!

### Split View
Want to see both at once? Click "Split View" to see your project and code side-by-side.

---

## 🎨 Using Templates

Not sure where to start? Use a template!

### How to Use Templates:

1. **Click "Browse Templates"** at the top
2. **Browse categories:**
   - Apps (calculator, todo list, timer)
   - Websites (portfolio, blog)
   - Games (quiz, memory game)
3. **Click "Use This Template"**
4. Your project is ready! Customize it with the AI assistant

### Popular Templates:
- 🧮 **Simple Calculator** - Perfect for beginners
- ✅ **Todo List** - Track your tasks
- 🌐 **Personal Website** - Show off your work
- 🎮 **Quiz Game** - Create fun quizzes
- ⏰ **Countdown Timer** - Track time

## 📤 Sharing Your Project

When you're happy with your project:

1. Click the blue **"Share"** button
2. A link will be copied to your clipboard
3. Send this link to anyone!

They can:
- View your project in their browser
- Use it without installing anything
- See your creation live!

## 🎓 Tips for Beginners

### Be Specific
The more details you provide, the better your project:
- ❌ "Make a website"
- ✅ "Create a personal website with my name, a photo, and three sections: About Me, My Projects, and Contact"

### Start Simple
Don't try to build everything at once:
1. Start with something small
2. Get it working
3. Ask AI to add more features one at a time

### Ask for Explanations
If you're curious about something:
- "Explain what this does"
- "How does this button work?"
- "What is this code doing?"

### Iterate and Improve
Your first version doesn't have to be perfect:
1. Create basic version
2. Test it
3. Ask AI: "Make it better"
4. Ask AI: "Make it prettier"

---

## 🆘 Need Help?

### If Something Doesn't Work
1. Try refreshing the page
2. Click "New Project" and start again
3. Ask the AI: "Fix the error"

### If You're Not Sure What to Build
- Click the example prompts on the homepage
- Browse templates for inspiration
- Think about what would make your life easier

### If AI Doesn't Understand
Try rephrasing your request:
- Be more specific
- Break it into smaller steps
- Use simpler words
